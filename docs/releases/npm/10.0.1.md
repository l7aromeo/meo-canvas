### Fixed

- A flex item told to ignore its own content took its size from the content
  anyway. A panel beside a fixed-height sibling came out at its content's
  height rather than the sibling's, so a 1024-tall image or a long block of
  text in a `flexGrow: 1` column pushed the whole row to 1024 where a browser
  gives 200.

  `flexBasis` with a minimum is what CSS provides for saying _ignore my
  content, take your size from the line_, and it now does that here. Both parts
  are needed and neither is enough alone: an automatic minimum floors the item
  at its content whatever the basis says.

  **Neither value has to be zero.** `flexBasis: 0` with `minHeight: 0` is the
  spelling most people write, but `flexBasis: 120` and `minHeight: 1` collapse
  the same way, and so does `minHeight: '0%'`. What does not collapse is
  `flexBasis: '0%'` — a percentage basis against a container with no height of
  its own means _auto_, so the content decides, and a browser agrees.

  A container with a stated height was never affected, which is why this
  survived: give the column a `height` and the same declarations always worked.
  (#145)
