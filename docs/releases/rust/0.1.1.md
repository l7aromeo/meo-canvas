### Fixed

- A flex item with a definite `flex_basis` and a definite minimum took its main
  size from its content rather than from its line. A `Box` beside a 200-tall
  sibling, in a column with no height of its own, came out at its content's
  1024 where Chrome gives 200 — so a panel grew to whatever it happened to
  hold.

  `flex_basis` with a minimum on the main axis is CSS's way of saying _ignore
  my content, take your size from the line_, and it now does that here. Both
  are required: `Dimension::Auto` on the minimum floors the item at its content
  whatever the basis says, which is why neither property worked alone.

  **Neither value has to be zero.** `Points(120.0)` collapses exactly as
  `Points(0.0)` does, and so does a minimum of `Percent(0.0)`. A basis of
  `Percent(0.0)` does not, because a percentage against a container with no
  definite main size resolves as `auto` and the content decides — Chrome agrees
  on both.

  A container with a stated main size was never affected, which is why this
  went unnoticed: the same declarations always worked there.

  **It is a workaround rather than a fix, and it is marked as one in the
  source.** `[WORKAROUND]` in `crates/meo-canvas-core/src/layout.rs` carries the
  derivation, and `crates/meo-canvas-core/tests/taffy_definite_basis.rs` fails
  the day the layout engine underneath stops needing it. No upstream issue
  covers this one, and the comment says so rather than citing a number that
  does not. (#145)
