### Fixed

- A flex item with a definite `flex_basis` and a definite minimum took its main
  size from its content rather than from its line. A `Box` beside a 200-tall
  sibling, in a column with no height of its own, came out at its content's
  1024 where Chrome gives 200 — so a panel grew to whatever it happened to
  hold.

  `flex_basis` with a minimum on the main axis is CSS's way of saying _ignore
  my content, take your size from the line_, and it now does that here. Both
  are required: `Dimension::Auto` on the minimum floors the item at its content
  whatever the basis says, which is why neither property worked alone.

  **Neither value has to be zero.** A basis of `Points(1.0)` or `Points(120.0)`
  collapses exactly as `Points(0.0)` does, and so do minimums of `Points(1.0)`
  and `Percent(0.0)`. A basis of `Percent(0.0)` does not, because a percentage
  against a container with no definite main size resolves as `auto` and the
  content decides — Chrome agrees on both.

  A container with a stated main size was never affected, which is why this
  went unnoticed: the same declarations always worked there.

  **It is a workaround rather than a fix, and it is marked as one in the
  source.** taffy builds a flex container's content size from what its items
  contain rather than from the hypothetical main sizes they asked for, and this
  crate supplies the right number before the solve. `[WORKAROUND]` in
  `crates/meo-canvas-core/src/layout.rs` carries the derivation, and
  `crates/meo-canvas-core/tests/taffy_definite_basis.rs` fails the day taffy
  stops needing it — so this behaviour can move again, toward the browser. No
  upstream issue covers this one, so there is no number to watch, and the
  comment says so rather than citing one that does not; the issue this
  repository filed stays open until taffy is fixed, by design rather than by
  oversight. (#145)
